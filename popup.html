About your API Key

Your API key is the only way to access your memories on ChainMemory. It is generated locally in your browser and is unique to you.

Important rules:
• Save it in a password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, etc.)
• Never share it publicly or post it on social media
• ChainMemory cannot recover it if lost — by design, there is no "forgot password" option
• If you lose it, you will not be able to access your saved memories (but they remain on the blockchain forever)

About your Wallet

Each API key has a unique blockchain wallet (0x...) associated with it. This wallet is the on-chain identity of your memories.

You can view your wallet activity at chainmemory.ai by searching for your wallet address.

What is permanent storage?

When you click "Save to ChainMemory":

1. A summary of the conversation is sent to the ChainMemory blockchain (Chain ID 202604)
2. It is stored permanently with your wallet as the owner
3. No one can delete or modify it — not even OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or ChainMemory itself
4. You can access it anytime from chainmemory.ai

What is memory injection? NEW in v2.1

When you visit ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity, a floating "Inject memory" button appears at the bottom-left of the page.

Click it to:
1. See a preview of your recent ChainMemory history
2. Confirm — your memory gets pasted at the top of the chat input
3. The AI you're talking to reads your verified history and gives continuity

This lets you carry your AI memory from any platform to any other — the same memory, anywhere.

FAQ

Is it really free? ›
Yes. You only need AIC tokens (the native gas token) to write to the blockchain, and the faucet gives them for free. The extension itself is free forever.
What if I lose my API key? ›
You will lose access to your saved memories. They will remain on the blockchain forever, but only the holder of the API key can save new ones under that wallet. Save your key in a password manager from day one.
Can I delete a memory? ›
No. By design, memories saved to ChainMemory are permanent and cannot be deleted by anyone — including you. This is the core promise of the project. Think before you click "Save".
What gets saved exactly? ›
Currently, a 280-character summary of the question and the AI's response is saved on-chain. We are exploring future tiers for saving full conversations.
Why blockchain instead of a normal database? ›
Because no central authority (including ChainMemory) can alter or delete your AI memories. They are owned by you, verifiable, and outlive any company.
Is the injected text visible to the AI provider? ›
Yes — the memory is pasted into the chat input, so the AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) processes it like any other text you type. Only inject memories you're comfortable sharing in that conversation.

Troubleshooting

"Save" button doesn't appear: Refresh the page. If still missing, the AI platform may have updated its layout — please report it.

"Inject memory" button doesn't appear: Make sure you're on a supported AI page (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity). The button is fixed at the bottom-left corner.

Injection didn't paste into the input: The platform may have changed its HTML. The extension automatically falls back to copying the context to your clipboard — just paste it manually.

"Insufficient funds" error: Claim free AIC tokens at the faucet.

"Not registered" error: The extension will auto-register you on first save. If it persists, disconnect and reconnect from the Setup tab.
